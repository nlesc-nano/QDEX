Excited states: BSE
===================

The excited states are built on the QP energies and the screened interaction W of
:doc:`/quasiparticles/index`. Reading order:

1. :doc:`foundations`: the TDA BSE, K\ :sup:`x` and K\ :sup:`d`, singlets, triplets and spinors.
2. :doc:`frameworks`: what each ``excitation_mode`` keeps of K\ :sup:`x` and K\ :sup:`d`.
3. :doc:`screened_kernel`: the BSE uses the W of the QP model; the kernel of each model and how it
   behaves.
4. :doc:`cancellation`: why ΔW in the QP gap and in K\ :sup:`d` does not cancel completely.
5. :doc:`solvers`, :doc:`model_selection` and :doc:`configuration`.
